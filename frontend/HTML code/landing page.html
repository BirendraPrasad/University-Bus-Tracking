Two kinds of buses, one live map

The fleet is split by purpose — long-haul routes and quick internal shuttles — both tracked the same way.

Route

Route Bus

Fixed routes with multiple predefined stops and live location tracking, connecting hostels, gates and departments across campus and the city.

  • Predefined stop sequence
  • Scheduled departures
  • Live GPS on every trip
Shuttle

Shuttle Bus

Short campus and internal routes with frequent service and live tracking, built for quick hops between blocks, hostels and the main gate.

  • Short internal loops
  • High-frequency service
  • Live GPS on every trip
